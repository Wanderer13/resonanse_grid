Two matching giraffes see each other — that is friendship. The number on the edge is the pair’s strength, not a cell count.

Meadow rules

Place giraffes of height 1, 2, and 3 so the numbers along the edges match. A number is the sum of pair friendships on that line, not the number of giraffes. Two threes score 3; three threes score 6.

  • Two matching heights on a line of sight: friendship, worth that height.
  • Different heights: confusion ~. That is not a mistake, and it does not count.
  • A tree breaks the gaze.
  • Click cycles a cell: empty → 1 → 2 → 3 → empty.
  • Hint reveals one cell from the solution.
